How it works

From checkout to your door in four steps.

  1. Create your account

    Add your name and phone number and choose a PIN. Your US and UK addresses are ready straight away.

  2. Shop online

    Buy from Amazon, eBay, Walmart, ASOS, Next or any store that ships within the US or UK. Use your JustBuy address at checkout.

  3. We bring it over

    Your parcel reaches our warehouse, travels to Tanzania and clears customs. Every update shows in My packages.

  4. Collect and pay

    Taxes, duties and our fee are worked out upfront. Shipping is $10 per ½ kg, paid on delivery.
