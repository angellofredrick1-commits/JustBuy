How it works

From online checkout to your hands in four steps

  1. Register free

    Fill in your name and phone number. You get our US and UK addresses straight away.

  2. Shop online

    Buy from Amazon, eBay, Walmart, ASOS, Next or any store that ships within the US or UK. Use your JustBuy address at checkout.

  3. We ship it to Tanzania

    Your parcel arrives at our warehouse, travels to Tanzania and goes through customs. You can follow it online.

  4. Collect and pay

    Taxes, duties and our fee are worked out upfront. Shipping is $10 per ½ kg, paid on delivery.
